The beach

Noppharat Thara is the long, calm beach at the western end of Ao Nang. Longtail boats wait on the sand and the limestone islands sit on the horizon.

The night market

Ao Nang Landmark Night Market is a four-minute walk: street food, drinks and stalls every evening.

The everyday things

A 24-hour 7-Eleven, Starbucks and KFC are all within three minutes on foot.
