Small rooms, big colours, and the sea across the road

The Beach Box is built from converted shipping containers, each painted its own colour and lined up under a red roof around a green courtyard. Every room has four beds and its own air conditioning, so you share with three people, not thirty.

Step out of the gate and you are on the beach road. The night market, cafés and a 24-hour 7-Eleven are a three-minute walk.

Four beds to a box

Book one bed or take the whole room with friends. Bathrooms are in a separate block a few steps from your door.
